Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our domain ownership, care plan terms, and hassle-free cancellation policy.

Do I have to buy my own domain name?

No, we handle all the technical heavy lifting. Your $1,500 build includes the registration of one standard domain name (up to $20/year value). We set it up and connect it to our servers, but you retain 100% legal ownership of the domain at all times.

What happens if I want to cancel my Care Plan?

You can cancel your managed hosting at any time with a simple written email notice. We will immediately stop billing and issue a prorated refund for any unused days in that month. Because you own the website, we will package all your site files into a neat .zip folder and send them to you.

How do I get my domain if I leave?

We make transferring out completely painless. If you cancel, we do an instant 'Account Push.' You simply create a free account with our domain registrar, and we push the domain directly into your account. You get immediate, full control without dealing with complex transfer codes, and we keep your site live on our servers for a 14-day grace period so you have plenty of time to set up your new hosting.
