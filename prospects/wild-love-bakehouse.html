North Knoxville Roots

Rooted in Passion, Butter & Craft

Founded by Shaun and Meg Parrish (the team behind Knoxville’s historic Old City Java), Wild Love Bakehouse was created to bring genuine, uncompromising European baking traditions to North Central Street.

  • 🌾

    No Shortcuts, No Commercial Mixes

    Every pastry, sourdough loaf, and cream is made completely from raw ingredients in our Happy Holler bakery kitchen.

  • 🧈

    European Cultured Butter

    Higher butterfat laminations produce the light, shattering crust and melt-in-your-mouth crumb that define world-class Viennoiserie.

  • ☕

    Specialty Coffee Tradition

    Two decades of coffee mastery ensures your morning Cortado, Flat White, or Honey-Lavender latte is dialed in with scientific precision.

“Baking is patience. When you bite into a croissant that has spent three days fermenting, laminating, and resting, you can taste the dedication of the hands that shaped it.”

Shaun & Meg Parrish
Founders & Bakers • Wild Love Bakehouse & Old City Java
